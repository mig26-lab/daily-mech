# Anthropic IPO Roadshow Targets Up to $1.4 Trillion Valuation Ahead of November 19 Nasdaq Debut

![Anthropic](https://cdn.pixabay.com/photo/2024/06/09/06/38/ai-generated-8818218_640.jpg)
*Image from Pixabay: Futuristic AI technology illustration*

Anthropic, the AI lab behind the Claude family of models, has set a price range of $70 to $78 per share for its highly anticipated initial public offering. The company plans to offer 825 million Class A shares, which at the top of the range would raise up to $64.35 billion and value the firm at approximately $1.404 trillion on a fully diluted basis. Trading is expected to begin on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT around November 19, 2026.

This valuation marks a significant increase from Anthropic's roughly $965 billion private valuation following its Series H round earlier in 2026, though it falls short of earlier market speculation that had pointed toward $1.5 trillion to $2 trillion. The roadshow comes amid broader scrutiny of AI valuations, including the recent leadership upheaval at NVIDIA, a key supplier and investor in the space.

## Key Terms of the Offering

Anthropic's updated S-1 filing outlines a dual structure. In addition to the public share sale, the company has arranged a $26 billion private placement of non-voting Class N stock at the IPO price with cornerstone investors. These include Google and NVIDIA (approximately $10 billion each), AMD, Broadcom, Micron, Microsoft, and TSMC. Total proceeds could reach as high as $90 billion or more when including the private placement and any underwriter greenshoe option.

Run-rate revenue has climbed sharply, reaching roughly $81 billion as of late October according to the filing. The company has also disclosed substantial compute commitments with partners including Amazon and Google. Governance features a Long-Term Benefit Trust that elects a board majority, limiting the influence of Class A shareholders.

## Market Context and Investor Demand

The IPO arrives roughly five months after SpaceX's record-breaking offering. Early indications from the roadshow suggest strong demand, with some reports that bankers are considering pricing above the stated range. Retail investors are expected to receive an unusually large allocation of around 20 percent.

Anthropic has positioned Claude as a leading model for enterprise coding, analysis, and agentic workflows. Recent product moves include Claude Meetings (joining Google Meet calls) and expanded self-hosted managed agents. The listing will test whether public markets continue to support the high valuations that have characterized the AI boom.

## Risks and Broader Implications

Potential headwinds include ongoing debates over AI safety and regulation, competition from OpenAI, Google DeepMind, and Chinese labs such as DeepSeek, and dependence on scarce GPU supply. NVIDIA's own leadership transition adds uncertainty to the hardware landscape that underpins frontier model training and inference.

If successful, the IPO would rank among the largest in history and provide Anthropic with a substantial war chest for further model development, safety research, and infrastructure. It also offers public investors direct exposure to one of the leading pure-play AI companies.
