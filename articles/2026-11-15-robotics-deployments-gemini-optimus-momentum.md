# Robotics Momentum Accelerates: Gemini Robotics 2.5, Tesla Optimus Plans, and New Industrial Deployments

![Humanoid robot](https://cdn.pixabay.com/photo/2025/02/11/05/55/ai-generated-9398115_640.jpg)
*Image from Pixabay: Futuristic humanoid robot illustration*

November 2026 has seen a cluster of advances moving humanoid and specialized robots from laboratories toward real-world use. Google DeepMind's Gemini Robotics 2.5 vision-language-action model has demonstrated strong results, including a 96 percent success rate on automotive parts-kitting tasks with Apptronik Apollo 2 humanoids on Mercedes-Benz production lines. Tesla continues outlining plans to put its Optimus humanoid into practical factory work by 2027, while other companies report inspection, logistics, and service deployments.

## Gemini Robotics 2.5 on the Factory Floor

Released around November 10, Gemini Robotics 2.5 is described as DeepMind's most capable VLA model to date. Evaluations across more than 200 tasks showed success-rate improvements of 30 to 100 percent versus prior baselines and 20 to 30 percent faster completion times. Demonstrations include delicate manipulation such as tying bows and using tools. The model supports multiple embodiments, from dual-arm systems to full humanoids, and pairs with embodied-reasoning components for longer-horizon planning. An open safety benchmark, ASIMOV-Jailbreak, accompanies the release.

## Tesla Optimus and OpenAI's Approach Shift

Tesla is targeting factory applications for Optimus in 2027, signaling a major manufacturer's move from prototype to production-oriented labor. Separately, reports indicate OpenAI has disbanded its dedicated robotics research team, relying instead on general-purpose models to operate robots. This reflects a broader trend toward applying large foundation models directly to physical systems rather than building specialized robotics stacks.

## Additional Industrial and Service Advances

- Gecko Robotics introduced Raptor, a crawling inspection robot that collects data several times faster than prior systems, with ADNOC as an early customer for pipeline and tank work.
- AGIBOT G2 robots completed thousands of tasks in a 24-hour Shanghai convenience-store trial.
- Other activity includes pharmacy kiosks, farm-to-construction hauling robots, and continued humanoid pilots in logistics and manufacturing.

These developments coincide with rising investment in physical AI and growing availability of simulation and world-action models such as Runway's Praxis-1.

## Outlook

While reliability, safety, and cost remain challenges, the combination of improved VLA models, targeted commercial pilots, and major manufacturers entering the field is accelerating practical robotics. The next 12 to 18 months will likely determine how quickly these systems move from supervised trials to broader autonomous operation.
