# Nvidia Reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO After 11 Days, and Now the Board Faces the Questions

![Close-up of an electronic circuit board](https://cdn.pixabay.com/photo/2014/09/20/13/52/board-453758_1280.jpg)

*Image credit: Pixabay (ID 453758), free to use under the Pixabay Content License. Illustrative image of computer hardware.*

Eleven days after he resigned, Jensen Huang is back in charge of Nvidia. On November 23, 2026, the chipmaker said its board had voted **unanimously** to reinstate Huang as president, chief executive officer and board director, effective immediately. The announcement came in a [press release filed with the SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000090/nov23pressrelease.htm). The reversal sent Nvidia shares up 31.5% and lifted the whole market. It also raised hard questions about the board's independence and about the White House's role.

DailyMech covered the start of this story earlier this month, when CFO Colette Kress stepped in as interim CEO. Here is how it ended, for now.

## What Nvidia Announced

According to Nvidia's [press release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000090/nov23pressrelease.htm):

- The board reinstated Huang after "a series of discussions and meetings" with him and said it was satisfied with his "candor and ability to lead."
- The board received input from "senior levels of the United States government" and said it was confident Huang's return "poses no national security risk."
- **Harvey C. Jones** stays on as board chair. **Colette Kress** goes back to being CFO full time.
- A committee of independent directors, with independent counsel, will continue investigating reports that Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort. Huang says he supports that investigation.
- A new **National Security and Export Control Committee**, made up only of independent directors, will oversee trade compliance and national security risks.
- Nvidia is searching for its first **Chief Compliance Officer**.

"I am honored to return to NVIDIA and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a new industrial revolution," Huang said. "I can't wait to get back to work."

## How It Unfolded

The timeline, drawn from [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/3fec1b0a85a0/technology/nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-ceo-2026-11-23), [AP](https://apnews.com/article/nvidia-board-jensen-huang-reinstated-trump-7b0af450cb10c489ae00c51ae14af58a) and [AFP](https://www.afp.com/en/nvidia-reinstates-huang-ceo-after-trump-intervention):

- **November 12:** Huang resigns after revelations about extramarital affairs, including one with a woman suspected of being a Chinese spy.
- **The following week:** Huang posts that he "would be honored to answer these calls and return as CEO" and admits "serious mistakes." On the quarterly earnings call, Jones says the board does "not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO."
- **Meanwhile:** Thousands of current and former Nvidia employees sign an open letter, and figures including Elon Musk publicly push for his return.
- **The weekend before November 23:** President Donald Trump says he lobbies the board "directly and indirectly."
- **November 23:** The board reverses course. Employees at the Santa Clara headquarters cheer Huang's return. "I'm going to be a better CEO the second time around," he tells them, per AFP.

## The Market Reaction

Huang's exit had hit markets hard. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/nvidia-jensen-huang-chips-return-trump-china-01ba8c83e7d7d4fa79c22ca5dbc47d22) reports Nvidia stock had fallen by about a third since November 9, and the S&P 500 dropped more than 10% from its recent high, the definition of a correction. That added to fears that an AI bubble was bursting.

The comeback reversed much of that:

- Nvidia shares closed **up 31.5%** on November 23, adding nearly **$1 trillion** in market value, according to [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/3fec1b0a85a0/technology/nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-ceo-2026-11-23).
- The Nasdaq rose more than 6%, per [AFP](https://www.afp.com/en/nvidia-reinstates-huang-ceo-after-trump-intervention), and the S&P 500 gained 4.7%, per AP.
- Nvidia is back to being the world's second-most valuable company, after Apple.

Reuters also notes that Huang's absence had put Nvidia's China business at risk. Some Chinese customers canceled or delayed orders worth billions of dollars.

## Trump's Role

Trump has openly taken credit. "The board decided to do what we wanted them to do. I believe my intervention was very important," he told CNBC, as quoted by [AP](https://apnews.com/article/nvidia-jensen-huang-chips-return-trump-china-01ba8c83e7d7d4fa79c22ca5dbc47d22). Asked whether the falling market influenced him, he said, "probably so."

Policy moves followed quickly. [AFP](https://www.afp.com/en/nvidia-reinstates-huang-ceo-after-trump-intervention) reports that the Commerce Department added **Megaspeed International** and four affiliates in Malaysia, Singapore and Thailand to its Entity List. It also added Singapore-based Apex Enterprise Solutions, an Alibaba subsidiary. Malaysia urged data-center operators to cut ties with seven companies, and Singapore agreed to cooperate with the US.

## The Board Under Scrutiny

Governance experts are now focused on the 10-person board. "To bow to pressure to bring someone back who has hurt the reputation of the company is crazy," Kathleen Day of Johns Hopkins Carey Business School told [AP](https://apnews.com/article/nvidia-board-jensen-huang-reinstated-trump-7b0af450cb10c489ae00c51ae14af58a). "The shareholders should demand a new board." Nada Kakabadse of Henley Business School argued that long board tenures weaken independence, and suggested a maximum of eight years.

AP also raises concerns about how Huang runs the company. About 60 people report directly to him, and many supply deals have rested on his personal handshake. Musk made that point as an argument for bringing him back: "Without Jensen, at least some of those critical deals will be lost." Congressional investigations, lawsuits and the board's own unfinished investigation could still threaten his position.

## Why It Matters

- **Key-person risk is real.** A near-$1 trillion swing based on one executive shows how much the AI buildout depends on Nvidia, and on Huang personally.
- **Politics and boardrooms are colliding.** Open White House involvement in a CEO decision sets a precedent investors and regulators will debate.
- **Compliance gets new focus.** New committees and a Chief Compliance Officer suggest Nvidia expects tougher national-security oversight.
- **The story isn't over.** The independent investigation will "proceed to conclusion," and what it finds could change things again.
