# Gemini 4 Hydrogen: Google’s Fast Daily Driver for Agentic Workflows

![AI Robot Hand Technology](https://cdn.pixabay.com/photo/2025/04/28/15/08/ai-9565202_640.jpg)
*Image: AI robotic hand. Source: Pixabay (free under Pixabay Content License)*

Google DeepMind launched Gemini 4 Hydrogen on November 12, 2026, positioning it as the everyday workhorse for coding, agentic tasks, and multimodal workflows. Unlike the deeper-reasoning frontier models Gemini 4 Carbon and Argon, Hydrogen prioritizes speed and efficiency while still delivering strong intelligence.

## Key Specifications
- **Context Window**: Unified 1,048,576-token (1M) window covering input, thinking, and output.
- **Thinking Levels**: Low, medium, high (default), and new xhigh.
- **Pricing**: Flat $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens across the full window. Implicit caching at significant discount.
- **Availability**: Gemini API, Gemini app, Google Search, Gemini for business, Google Antigravity, and more.
- **Knowledge Cutoff**: Approximately March 2026.
- **Performance**: Scores 51 on Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at high thinking (54 at xhigh), surpassing Argon’s 50. Terminal-Bench 4.1: 57% (vs. 18.2% for Gemini 3.8 Flash). 2.5x faster than previous Flash at default settings, with 5x fewer fabricated claims.

Hydrogen is adapted from Gemini 4 Carbon and supports text, images, audio, and video. It handles Office and OpenDocument files natively and offers strong agentic coding, knowledge work (Agents’ Last Exam 71.6%), and computer use capabilities.

## Why It Matters
The model addresses the growing demand for cost-effective, high-volume agent execution. Partners report 2x–2.8x speed gains and 12–23% token reductions versus prior generations. It leads or ties on several professional benchmarks including Vals Tax and Legal Research.

In a week when AI infrastructure debt approaches $687 billion and hyperscalers commit trillions, efficient models like Hydrogen help control costs while expanding what agents can reliably do.

## Implications for Developers and Enterprises
Teams migrating from Gemini 3.8 Flash gain major leaps in both speed and reliability. The four thinking levels allow precise control over latency and cost. Combined with multi-model orchestration in Antigravity, Hydrogen serves as the fast worker alongside deeper models.
