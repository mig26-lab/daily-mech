# Google Launches Gemini 4 Neon and Makes Its Tasks Agent Free in Six Countries

![Abstract blue digital circuit pattern with circular rings](https://cdn.pixabay.com/photo/2018/05/14/16/54/cyber-3400789_1280.jpg)

*Image credit: Pixabay (image 3400789), free to use under the Pixabay Content License.*

Google made two moves in two days that push its Gemini lineup further into agent territory. On November 17 it made **Tasks**, its background AI agent formerly called Spark, free in six countries. On November 18 it released **Gemini 4 Neon**, a new mid-tier model it says offers frontier-level reasoning at Flash speeds, according to [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/18/gemini-4-neon-announcement/).

Neon is Google DeepMind's fourth model in just seven weeks, [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/ja/articles/gemini-4-neon-intelligence-gain) notes, following last week's Gemini 4 Carbon and Hydrogen releases.

## Gemini 4 Neon at a Glance

Neon sits between Carbon (the top tier) and Hydrogen (the fast, cheap tier). Google says it performs comparably to, or better than, Carbon on coding, reasoning and agentic tasks, but at Flash speeds and lower cost, per [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/18/gemini-4-neon-announcement/).

Key specs, according to [TestingCatalog](https://www.testingcatalog.com/google-launches-gemini-4-neon-on-gemini-antigravity-and-apis/) and [Android Headlines](https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/11/google-gemini-4-neon-release-pricing-features.html):

- **Context window:** 1 million tokens, with spreadsheets, PDFs, charts, audio and video as inputs.
- **Thinking levels:** four, from Low to Extra High (xhigh).
- **Coding benchmark:** 65.4% on CursorBench 4.1.
- **Agent role:** in Google Antigravity, Neon can act as an orchestrator that directs parallel Hydrogen subagents.

For now, Neon costs more per token than Carbon's discounted rate. Once Carbon goes to standard pricing on December 1, Neon becomes about 25% cheaper.

## Independent Testing

[Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/ja/articles/gemini-4-neon-intelligence-gain) found Neon delivers "a small Intelligence gain over Gemini 4 Carbon" at lower cost:

- **Cost per task:** $6.44, about 17% less than Carbon's $7.76 at non-discounted pricing.
- **Time per task:** about 21 minutes, roughly 40% faster than Carbon's 36 minutes.

That is a modest capability bump, not a leap. The more notable gain is speed and cost on long, multi-step work.

## Where You Can Use Neon

Neon is rolling out in the following places, per [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/frontier/ai/google-rolls-out-gemini-4-neon-for-complex-ai-work) and [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/18/gemini-4-neon-announcement/):

- **Gemini app:** for Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers on mobile, desktop and web. It replaces Gemini 4 Argon and retires Gemini 3.1 Pro.
- **Developer tools:** the paid Gemini API in Google AI Studio and Android Studio.
- **Business and coding:** Gemini for business, and Google Antigravity for paid Pro and Ultra subscribers.

The Gemini app's model menu is simplified to four options: Auto (the renamed 3.5 Flash-Lite), Hydrogen, Neon and Carbon, according to [Android Headlines](https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/11/google-gemini-4-neon-release-pricing-features.html). [TestingCatalog](https://www.testingcatalog.com/google-launches-gemini-4-neon-on-gemini-antigravity-and-apis/) adds that eligible members of Google's Fairwind Program will get a cyber-defense version of Neon over the next few days.

## Tasks: Google's Agent Goes Free

A day earlier, Google removed the paywall from **Tasks** in the Gemini app. It previously required a Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription. Free Tasks runs on Gemini 4 Hydrogen and is available in the **US, Japan, South Korea, Canada, Australia and India**, per [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/17/gemini-tasks-free/). Paid subscribers can now run Tasks on Neon for tougher, multi-step jobs.

Tasks began as **Gemini Spark**, shown at Google I/O in May 2026 as a 24/7 background agent running on dedicated Google virtual machines. It was folded into the main Gemini app in October, according to [AIPopDB](https://aipopdb.com/news/gemini-tasks-free).

### How It Works

- Open Gemini and switch the toggle from **Chat** to **Task**, per [Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/cool-tech/google-gemini-tasks-free/).
- Gemini works through jobs such as booking appointments, managing bills and ordering groceries, using your email, calendar and payment methods, per [iThinkDiff](https://www.ithinkdiff.com/gemini-tasks-free-six-countries/).
- Free users can run **up to 15 simultaneous cloud tasks**, with integrations across Workspace and more than 60 connected apps. Google hasn't disclosed daily or monthly quotas, according to [Softonic](https://en.softonic.com/articles/google-gemini-tasks-goes-free-in-six-countries-with-us-only-extras).

### The Fine Print

- **US-English-only extras:** calling businesses, Calendar scheduling, reservations, Google Pay checkout, Password Manager sign-ins, emailing instructions to Gemini and messaging through RCS or iMessage, per [Softonic](https://en.softonic.com/articles/google-gemini-tasks-goes-free-in-six-countries-with-us-only-extras).
- **State limits:** calling businesses isn't available in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana and Nebraska, per [Phandroid](https://phandroid.com/2026/11/18/geminis-ai-agent-no-longer-needs-a-paid-subscription-in-six-countries/).
- **Account rules:** you must be 18 or older and use a personal Google account with Gemini history turned on. Work and school accounts are excluded, per [The Agent Log](https://theagentlog.com/updates/2026-11-17-gemini-tasks-free-rollout/).
- **Not available** in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland or Nigeria.

## Why It Matters

- **Agents are becoming a free default.** Making Tasks free for what Google calls "hundreds of millions of people" puts pressure on rivals that still keep background agents behind paid plans.
- **The middle tier is where the action is.** Neon, OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Sol and Microsoft's MAI Code 2 all target "almost frontier, much cheaper" performance for everyday work.
- **Release cadence is accelerating.** Four Gemini models in seven weeks means teams need to re-test their model choices far more often.

**Helpful tip:** If you try free Gemini Tasks, start with low-risk jobs such as research summaries or price-drop alerts before connecting payment methods. Review what the agent did after each task, and keep purchase approvals turned on.
