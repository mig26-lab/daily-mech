# ChatGPT Wallet: OpenAI Enables Secure Agentic Purchases for Pro Users

![AI Agent Shopping](https://cdn.pixabay.com/photo/2024/05/08/07/50/ai-generated-8747658_1280.jpg)
*Illustration of AI technology from Pixabay*

On November 13, 2026, OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet, a new feature that allows its Dots AI agents to complete purchases on behalf of users. Available initially to U.S. ChatGPT Pro subscribers aged 18 and older with eligible cards from American Express, Mastercard, Visa, or Chase, the wallet prioritizes security by never sharing actual card details with agents or merchants.

## How It Works

Users add a card once to the wallet. When a Dot agent identifies a purchase—such as shopping for a product or booking travel—the user reviews the items, total, and selected card. Approval requires a biometric security check like Face ID or fingerprint scan. The payment network then issues a token that substitutes for the real card number, ensuring privacy while preserving rewards and dispute protections.

OpenAI partnered with major card networks to build this system. The company emphasizes that every transaction creates a verifiable record of user authorization. This design addresses concerns about AI agents making unauthorized buys, a issue that has led some airlines and retailers to restrict third-party agents.

## Broader Context in Agentic AI

This launch follows OpenAI's introduction of Dots, always-on personal agents, and earlier tools like ChatGPT Finance for bank connections. It positions ChatGPT to compete in agentic commerce, where AI not only recommends but executes transactions. Similar efforts from other firms, including Meta's Muse agents using Stripe, highlight growing industry focus on trusted AI payments.

Availability starts limited to gather real-world feedback before wider expansion. OpenAI notes that some websites may still block agent activity due to fraud systems or policies, as seen with United Airlines restrictions.

## Implications

ChatGPT Wallet lowers friction for AI-assisted shopping while maintaining user control. For businesses, it opens opportunities to integrate via plugins or WebMCP tools. As agents handle more complex tasks, secure payment layers become essential infrastructure.

The feature underscores OpenAI's push toward practical, everyday AI utility beyond chat. With general models improving, capabilities like this could accelerate adoption of personal AI agents for routine decisions and actions.
