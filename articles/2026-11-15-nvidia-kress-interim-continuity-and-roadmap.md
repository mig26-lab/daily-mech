# NVIDIA’s Leadership Transition: Colette Kress as Interim CEO and Continuity in AI Hardware

**Published: November 15, 2026**

NVIDIA, the company that has powered much of the modern AI boom through its GPUs and software stack, entered a new chapter on November 12, 2026. Founder and longtime CEO Jensen Huang resigned as President, Chief Executive Officer, and board member, effective immediately. The board appointed Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress as interim CEO while she continues in her CFO role, and named longtime director Harvey C. Jones as the company’s first-ever Chair of the Board. The move followed a period of intense scrutiny involving personal matters and national security questions, triggering significant stock volatility and raising questions about the company’s product roadmap, open-source commitments, and China-related business.

### Background of the Transition
Huang co-founded NVIDIA in 1993 and led it for more than three decades, transforming it from a graphics chip maker into one of the world’s most valuable companies and a central infrastructure provider for generative AI. In early November 2026, reports emerged of extramarital relationships, including one involving a woman U.S. authorities reportedly suspected of ties to Chinese intelligence, and another with a senior company executive. NVIDIA’s board launched an independent investigation. After further developments, the board accepted Huang’s resignation on November 11. In a regulatory filing and press release, the company stated that operations, customer commitments, and the product roadmap would continue without interruption.

Shares fell sharply in the immediate aftermath—reports indicated drops of 4% to more than 12% on the day of the announcement, with larger cumulative declines earlier in the week that erased hundreds of billions in market value. Broader market effects appeared as well, given NVIDIA’s outsized role in AI infrastructure indices and supply chains.

### Colette Kress and Operational Continuity
Colette Kress has served as Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer since 2013. She is widely regarded as deeply knowledgeable about the company’s finances, customer relationships, and Wall Street communications. Her dual role as interim CEO and continuing CFO is intended to provide stability while the board conducts a search for a permanent successor that will consider both internal and external candidates. The board has emphasized that existing commitments to customers and the product timeline remain intact.

NVIDIA is scheduled to report fiscal results in the near term (around November 17 in some reporting). Investors and partners will watch closely for any signals on demand, inventory, and the pace of next-generation accelerators (including Blackwell-class and subsequent platforms). Analysts have noted that Kress and other long-tenured executives, such as those in engineering leadership, could help maintain momentum even as the search for a permanent CEO proceeds.

### Implications for AI Hardware, Robotics, and the Ecosystem
NVIDIA’s GPUs and software (CUDA, Omniverse, Isaac, GR00T robotics models, etc.) underpin training and inference for frontier models, as well as an increasing number of robotics and physical AI systems. A leadership transition at this scale introduces uncertainty around:
- Export controls and China market access, an area in which Huang had been personally active.
- Open-source and developer ecosystem priorities, including any pending or reported deals involving platforms such as Hugging Face.
- Partnerships with robotics companies deploying humanoids and industrial systems that rely on NVIDIA platforms for simulation, training, and edge inference.
- Capital allocation for future process nodes, networking, and software stacks.

Reports have also linked the broader environment to questions about Chinese labs’ access to restricted high-end chips. While the board has stated that national security and company secrets were not believed to be compromised in the immediate aftermath of the initial reports, ongoing congressional and regulatory attention is likely.

### Outlook
In the short term, the focus is on operational continuity under Kress and Jones. Longer term, the permanent CEO selection will shape NVIDIA’s strategic posture on open ecosystems, international markets, and the balance between data-center accelerators and emerging physical AI workloads. The AI industry’s dependence on a relatively concentrated set of hardware suppliers means any sustained disruption—or even prolonged uncertainty—could influence model training costs, robotics deployment timelines, and competitive dynamics among cloud providers and model labs.

NVIDIA remains the dominant provider of the compute that has enabled rapid progress in large language models, vision systems, and embodied AI. The transition tests whether that infrastructure layer can maintain its pace under new leadership while navigating heightened geopolitical and governance scrutiny.
