# Wing Launches Free and Paid Walmart Drone Deliveries in Memphis Metro

**Published: November 13, 2026**

![Delivery drone carrying package](https://cdn.pixabay.com/photo/2026/07/28/01/49/01-49-06-375_640.jpg)
*Image from Pixabay (free under Pixabay Content License)*

## Introduction

Alphabet’s Wing began drone deliveries of Walmart orders in the Memphis metro area on November 10, 2026, from three Supercenters: Bartlett and Cordova in Tennessee, and West Memphis in Arkansas. This marks Wing’s first Tennessee market and its sixth U.S. metro with Walmart, expanding the network toward a 2027 goal of more than 270 stores serving tens of millions of people.

Customers order via the Walmart app, Walmart.com, or the Wing app for items up to 2.75 pounds (expanding to 5 pounds soon), with deliveries in as little as 30 minutes. The drone hovers around 23 feet and lowers packages on a tether.

## Service Details and Pricing

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Launch date | November 10, 2026 |
| Stores | 3 Supercenters (Bartlett, Cordova, West Memphis) |
| Payload | 2.75 lb currently; 5 lb planned |
| Speed / altitude | Up to 60 mph, cruise 150–200 ft |
| Pricing | Reports vary: ~$19.99 or free for Walmart+ / limited promotions; check at checkout |
| Coverage | Tens of thousands of homes |

Wing is investing up to $125 million in a Fort Worth factory to scale aircraft production. The service reduces neighborhood traffic for last-mile goods while operating under existing FAA rules for beyond-visual-line-of-sight where authorized.

## Broader Expansion Context

Walmart and Wing already operate in Atlanta, Charlotte, Dallas-Fort Worth, Houston, and Orlando, with more metros announced (including Los Angeles, Seattle, Denver, and others). Memphis is the first of a June 2026 announced batch to go live. Amazon has shown interest in the market but has not launched competing service yet.

## Conclusion

The Memphis launch demonstrates continued scaling of commercial drone delivery beyond pilot stages. Reliability, weather limits, and consistent pricing will determine how quickly it becomes routine. Residents in range should check eligibility in the Walmart or Wing apps.
