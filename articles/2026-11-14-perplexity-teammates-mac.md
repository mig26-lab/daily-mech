## Introduction

Perplexity has expanded its Teammates feature—persistent AI agents designed for ongoing work—to the company’s Mac app, available exclusively to Max subscribers. Announced on X on November 13, 2026, the desktop rollout follows the initial web research preview that launched earlier in the month (around November 4–5). Teammates take on tasks individually or in collaboration, handling areas such as QA, customer service, research, growth marketing, and more, while keeping a single persistent thread and notifying users only when decisions are required.

This moves Perplexity further from pure search into agentic workflows that continue after the user steps away from the keyboard.

## How Teammates Work

Each Teammate operates with its own isolated desktop and browser session, maintaining independent cookies and context. They build on Perplexity Computer capabilities, can connect to tools including Slack, GitHub, Linear, Gmail, and others, and remember goals, history, and shared files across runs. Users set them up by naming the agent after a project, connecting tools, and describing ownership of deliverables and timing. Corrections in chat are applied to future work. Stronger models can be used for planning while faster ones handle routine steps.

Multiple Teammates can collaborate within a Project. The feature remains a research preview, so behavior may evolve. Access is limited to individual Max plans at $200 per month or $2,000 per year. Pro users and most enterprise plans are excluded by default (enterprise access requires admin request). A promotional period offers free Teammate usage; afterward, activity draws from monthly Max credits.

## Market Context and Implications

Teammates compete in the growing space of background and multi-agent systems alongside offerings from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and startups. Desktop availability is particularly relevant for knowledge workers who spend significant time on Macs. By limiting early access to the highest tier, Perplexity can manage compute costs while gathering feedback from power users.

Potential use cases include monitoring on-call issues, managing ad campaigns, drafting research summaries, or coordinating cross-tool workflows. Challenges remain around reliability, credit consumption for complex tasks, and governance for production access.

## Conclusion

Perplexity’s Mac release of Teammates gives Max subscribers a native desktop interface for persistent AI agents that own ongoing work rather than answering one-off prompts. While still in research preview and restricted to the $200/month tier, it accelerates the shift toward agentic productivity tools. Users should monitor credit usage and preview changes as the product matures.
