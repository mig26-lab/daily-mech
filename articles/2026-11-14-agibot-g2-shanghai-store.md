# AgiBot G2 Humanoids Log 1,730 Tasks in 24-Hour Shanghai Convenience Store Trial

![Futuristic humanoid robot in tech environment](https://cdn.pixabay.com/photo/2024/02/18/11/11/ai-generated-8581008_1280.jpg)
*Image from Pixabay under free license*

On November 2-3, 2026, six AGIBOT G2 wheeled humanoid robots completed a full 24-hour shift at an operating convenience store in Shanghai’s Xuhui District. Together they handled 1,730 tasks ranging from customer greetings and coffee preparation to shelf restocking and table cleaning. Four of the six robots passed the company’s final performance assessment. The livestream drew more than two million viewers and marked the conclusion of AGIBOT’s AI WAVE technology showcase.

## Key Results

| Task Category | Number Completed |
|---------------|------------------|
| Customer-service interactions | 683 |
| Product pickups and deliveries | 369 |
| Coffee orders | 147 |
| Breakfast servings | 126 |
| Shelf inspections and restocking | 277 |
| Table-cleaning tasks | 128 |
| **Total** | **1,730** |

The trial covered lunchtime rush, evening peak, overnight operations, and the next morning’s breakfast rush. Robots were assigned specialized roles: greeter, barista, shelf patrol and restocking, checkout/hot food, and cleaner.

## What the Trial Revealed

The robots demonstrated competence in structured, repetitive work. They distinguished regular from sugar-free beverages, restored scrambled shelf items by visual counting rather than price tags, and navigated around temporary obstacles. Fine manipulation remained challenging — hanging bags on hooks or handling soft cake without damage produced failures.

Two robots did not pass assessment. The shelf-patrol unit was judged insufficiently proactive, and the cleaning robot left its assigned area after encountering a full bin and failed to return. These outcomes highlight remaining gaps in exception handling and long-horizon autonomy in unstructured environments.

## Implications for Embodied AI

- **Real-world duration**: A continuous 24-hour public deployment is rarer than short demos and exposes compounding small errors.
- **Multi-robot coordination**: AGIBOT’s Genie Master framework linked task planning, motion, and feedback across the fleet.
- **Customer-facing retail**: Success in coffee service and product handoff shows progress toward practical service robots, yet the pass rate (4/6) and missing published success/takeover metrics leave questions about reliability.
- **Data flywheel**: Company statements emphasize that logged failures improve subsequent training runs.

This trial sits alongside other late-2026 humanoid factory and retail pilots (including Apptronik Apollo 2 on Mercedes-Benz lines with Gemini Robotics 2.5). Together they indicate that general-purpose humanoids are moving from controlled labs into live commercial settings, even if full unsupervised reliability remains incomplete.
