## Introduction

On November 12, 2026, Nvidia announced that co-founder and longtime CEO Jensen Huang had resigned as president, CEO, and board member, effective immediately. The board appointed Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress as interim CEO while she continues in her CFO role, and named longtime director Harvey C. Jones as the company’s first-ever board chair. The transition follows weeks of scrutiny over Huang’s personal conduct and potential national-security implications.

Nvidia shares fell sharply on the news, with reports of double-digit declines and hundreds of billions in market-cap impact in the preceding days.

## Background of the Resignation

Huang, who led Nvidia for 33 years and transformed it into the dominant supplier of AI accelerators, admitted to extramarital relationships. Reports linked one relationship to an individual U.S. intelligence officials identified as a suspected Chinese spy, and another to a senior Nvidia executive. The board initiated an independent investigation and ultimately accepted his resignation.

The company emphasized that product roadmaps, customer commitments, and operations would continue without interruption.

## Colette Kress as Interim Leader

Kress has served as Nvidia’s CFO since 2013, a period during which annual revenue grew from roughly $4 billion to more than $200 billion. She is well-known to investors and has deep operational knowledge of the business. Internal memos from Kress and Jones reassured employees that the company would hold a meeting on November 18 to discuss the future and that a search for a permanent CEO (internal and external candidates) is underway.

## Implications for AI and Robotics

Nvidia is central to the AI infrastructure boom and has expanded aggressively into robotics platforms (Isaac, GR00T, Omniverse). Leadership continuity matters for hyperscaler partnerships, sovereign AI projects, and physical AI initiatives. Analysts and the board have stressed that the product roadmap remains intact; however, questions remain about whether a finance-oriented interim CEO versus an engineering-focused permanent successor will affect long-term technical priorities.

The company is scheduled to report fiscal results on November 17, providing an early test of market confidence under the new leadership structure.

## Conclusion

The sudden departure of Jensen Huang marks the end of an era at Nvidia. Colette Kress brings continuity and financial discipline as interim CEO, while the board begins a structured search for a permanent leader. For the AI and robotics ecosystems that depend on Nvidia silicon and software, the immediate priority is stable execution; the longer-term question is how the next permanent CEO will balance the company’s dual role as both AI infrastructure provider and emerging physical-AI platform.
