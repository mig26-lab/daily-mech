# NVIDIA Leadership Transition: Colette Kress Steps In as Interim CEO After Jensen Huang Resigns

![NVIDIA technology](https://cdn.pixabay.com/photo/2023/05/24/17/49/ai-generated-8015427_640.jpg)
*Image from Pixabay: Futuristic robot technology illustration*

On November 12, 2026, NVIDIA co-founder and longtime CEO Jensen Huang resigned as president, chief executive, and board member, effective immediately. The board appointed Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress as interim CEO while she continues in her finance role, and named former Synopsys executive Harvey C. Jones as the company's first-ever board chair. A search is underway for a permanent successor, considering both internal and external candidates.

Huang's departure followed revelations of personal relationships, including one involving a woman U.S. authorities suspect of spying for China, and a second with a senior NVIDIA executive. The board had launched an investigation, and Huang publicly apologized for personal conduct before resigning. Shares fell sharply in the immediate aftermath, at one point erasing hundreds of billions in market value.

## Implications for AI Hardware and Robotics

NVIDIA has been the central supplier of GPUs powering the AI boom, from training frontier models to robotics platforms such as Isaac and GR00T. Huang's close ties with the U.S. administration and his advocacy for continued China market access had shaped export-control debates. His exit raises questions about continuity of product roadmaps (including Blackwell and subsequent generations), customer commitments, and the company's stance on open-source AI efforts and robotics software.

Colette Kress, who has served as CFO for more than a decade, is viewed as a stabilizing figure with deep Wall Street relationships. Other internal candidates mentioned by observers include longtime engineering leaders. External names have circulated in speculation, though none have been confirmed.

## Broader Ecosystem Effects

Chinese labs have reportedly continued training advanced models on restricted hardware, increasing political pressure on U.S. export policy. NVIDIA's earnings report scheduled for November 17 will be closely watched for any guidance changes under interim leadership. The transition also affects partners such as Anthropic, which counts NVIDIA among its cornerstone IPO investors, and numerous robotics companies relying on NVIDIA platforms.

Industry reaction has ranged from concern over short-term disruption to confidence that NVIDIA's engineering culture and installed base will sustain momentum. The company stated that operations, customer commitments, and the product roadmap are expected to continue without interruption.

## Looking Ahead

A permanent CEO appointment will be critical for long-term strategy in physical AI, robotics, and the next generation of accelerated computing. Until then, Kress's dual role provides continuity while the board conducts its search. Investors and customers will monitor the November 17 earnings and subsequent announcements for signals on strategic priorities.
