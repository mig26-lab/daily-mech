# Gemini Robotics 2.5 and the Embodied AI Factory Wave

![Factory Robot](https://cdn.pixabay.com/photo/2024/06/29/11/00/ai-generated-8861233_640.jpg)
*Image: Industrial robot on factory floor. Source: Pixabay*

Google DeepMind released Gemini Robotics 2.5 on November 10, 2026, its most advanced vision-language-action (VLA) model. The update delivers 30–100% higher success rates and 20–30% faster completion across more than 200 tasks compared with the prior version, including tool use, deformable object handling, and precision assembly.

## Factory Proof of Concept
In pilots with Apptronik’s Apollo 2 humanoid on Mercedes-Benz production lines (Hungary and Germany), the system achieved a 96% success rate on parts kitting. The model also improves safety near obstacles and people and ships with the open-source ASIMOV-Jailbreak benchmark for testing adversarial prompts.

Gemini Robotics 2.5 controls a wide range of embodiments—from bi-arms to full humanoids—with whole-body intelligence, advanced dexterity (including 22-DoF hands), and on-the-fly adaptation.

## Parallel Progress: Embodied AI’s AIR Humanoid
Swiss startup Embodied AI unveiled AIR, a wheeled, tendon-driven humanoid designed for factory floors. It learns changing tasks rapidly—reaching 98% reliability on cable handling after short operator guidance—and was developed from research to industrial product in nine months. The company has signed contracts with major OEMs and is moving toward scaled production. AIR addresses labor shortages (estimated 1.9 million unfilled U.S. manufacturing jobs by 2033) without requiring full facility redesigns.

## Broader Context
South Korea leads global robot density (1,066 industrial robots per 10,000 workers) and is investing ~$330 million to localize humanoid components. Other advances include Avride’s one million robot deliveries, Serve Robotics opening its delivery API, and Wing/Walmart drone service in Memphis.

Together, specialized VLA models like Gemini Robotics 2.5 and on-the-job learning platforms like AIR are accelerating the shift from rigid automation to flexible, collaborative physical AI.
