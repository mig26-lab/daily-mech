# Anthropic IPO Roadshow: $70-$78 Range Targets Up to $1.4 Trillion Valuation Ahead of Expected Nov 19 Nasdaq Debut

![Stock market chart](https://cdn.pixabay.com/photo/2016/11/23/14/37/blur-1853262_1280.jpg)
*Image from Pixabay: Stock market trading screen*

Anthropic, the developer of the Claude family of AI models, has set a price range of $70 to $78 per share for its initial public offering of 825 million Class A shares. At the top of the range, the offering would raise up to approximately $64.35 billion and value the company at roughly $1.4 trillion on a fully diluted basis. Trading is expected to begin on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT around November 19, 2026, following pricing on or about November 18, according to reports citing people familiar with the process.

The company also arranged a concurrent private placement of $26 billion in non-voting Class N stock at the IPO price with cornerstone investors including Google, Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, Microsoft, Micron, and TSMC. Google and Nvidia are each investing around $10 billion in this placement. Total net proceeds to Anthropic, including the IPO and private placement (and potentially the underwriters’ overallotment option), are estimated in the range of $84–93 billion.

## Key Terms and Market Context

The amended S-1 filing on November 10 outlined the offering structure. Anthropic is selling the majority of the shares, with a smaller portion from existing stockholders. The Long-Term Benefit Trust retains significant governance influence. Retail allocation is targeted at around 20 percent through platforms including Charles Schwab, Fidelity, Robinhood, and others—higher than the typical 5–10 percent for IPOs.

This valuation sits below earlier reports of $1.5–2 trillion targets discussed months earlier but represents a substantial step up from Anthropic’s May 2026 funding round at $965 billion. The IPO comes amid broader market scrutiny of AI infrastructure spending and follows SpaceX’s large offering earlier in 2026. Anthropic’s run-rate revenue has been reported in the tens of billions of dollars range, though compute commitments remain substantial.

## Implications for the AI Industry

A successful listing would provide Anthropic with significant capital for continued model training, safety research, and expansion of Claude products, including agentic features. It also creates a public benchmark for pure-play frontier AI labs. Nvidia’s participation as a cornerstone investor is notable given the recent leadership transition at the chipmaker; the deal was structured prior to Jensen Huang’s resignation.

Market reaction to the roadshow has been described as strong, with reports of the institutional book being oversubscribed. Demand may even support pricing above the initial range, according to some coverage. Investors will watch for final pricing, first-day trading performance, and any updates on model roadmaps or partnership commitments in the prospectus and subsequent communications.

## Looking Ahead

The debut will test investor appetite for AI companies at trillion-dollar scales amid ongoing debates over energy costs, competition from Google, OpenAI, and Chinese labs, and regulatory developments. For the broader ecosystem, a well-received IPO could encourage further capital formation in AI infrastructure and applications. Conversely, any volatility would signal caution on valuations.
