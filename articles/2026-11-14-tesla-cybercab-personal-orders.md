# Tesla Opens Personal Cybercab Orders at $29,990 Plus Required $299 Monthly Autonomy Subscription

![Futuristic robot cityscape representing autonomous mobility](https://cdn.pixabay.com/photo/2024/02/06/10/13/ai-generated-8556594_1280.jpg)
*Image from Pixabay under free license*

On November 13, 2026, Tesla’s official Robotaxi account announced that select customers are being invited to purchase the Cybercab for personal use. The two-seat, steering-wheel-free vehicle is listed at $29,990 before taxes, with an estimated delivery window of December 2026 in Austin, Texas. A $1,000 non-refundable deposit is required to order.

## Pricing and Operating Requirements

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Purchase price | $29,990 (excludes taxes) |
| Autonomy Subscription | $299 per month (required to operate) |
| Included months | First month free with purchase |
| Order deposit | $1,000 non-refundable |
| Estimated delivery | December 2026 (Austin, TX) |
| Seating | Two seats |
| Storage | 20.1 cubic feet |
| Key restriction | Operates only in supported Robotaxi service areas |

The subscription is pre-selected on the order page. Fine print states that both the subscription and regular vehicle maintenance are required for operation. Owners can monitor the vehicle and manage rides through Tesla’s app, but the Cybercab has no manual driving controls.

## Geographic and Practical Limits

Operation is limited to Tesla’s current Robotaxi geofences (Austin, Dallas, Houston, Fort Worth, San Antonio in Texas, and Miami, Orlando, Tampa in Florida as of the announcement). Outside these zones the vehicle cannot drive itself. Early invites appear targeted at customers already in or near active service areas, particularly Austin.

This model represents Tesla’s push toward Level 4 autonomous personal vehicles. The purchase price undercuts the Model 3, yet the ongoing $299 monthly fee (approximately $3,588 annually after the free month) plus insurance and maintenance creates a different total cost of ownership profile than conventional Teslas.

## Broader Context

- Production of Cybercab began at Gigafactory Texas earlier in 2026; the company has indicated plans to free capacity by winding down Cybertruck output around mid-2027.
- The vehicle is positioned both for personal ownership and potential Robotaxi network participation.
- Federal regulatory scrutiny of autonomous vehicles continues in parallel with the sales push.

The launch of personal orders marks a concrete step from robotaxi pilots toward consumer ownership of fully autonomous vehicles, while the geofence and subscription requirements underscore that unsupervised operation remains geographically constrained.
