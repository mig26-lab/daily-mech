# Gemini Robotics 2.5: DeepMind Delivers Measurable Gains on the Factory Floor

![Humanoid robot interacting with technology](https://cdn.pixabay.com/photo/2024/03/13/17/57/artificial-intelligence-8631494_640.jpg)

Google DeepMind released Gemini Robotics 2.5 on November 10, 2026, its latest vision-language-action model for controlling robots in dynamic environments. Tested on over 200 tasks, it shows 30–100% higher success rates and 20–30% faster execution than prior versions, with gains in dexterity, reliability, and safety.

Safety improvements include 33% more successful completions near obstacles and 43% near people. The model handles delicate objects, deformable materials, and precise actions such as pipetting or tying bows.

## Mercedes-Benz Production Pilot

A key proof-of-concept used Apptronik Apollo 2 humanoids on Mercedes-Benz lines in Kecskemét, Hungary, and Sindelfingen, Germany. For automotive parts kitting—picking brackets from bins and placing them into trays—the system achieved 96% success. This moves humanoids from demos into repetitive industrial work, freeing humans for higher-skill tasks.

The model works natively across platforms, including Apollo 2’s multi-DoF hands and dual-arm systems like Franka Duo. Development accelerated via Gemini 4 Argon for simulation and analysis, completing in roughly four months.

## Safety Benchmarks and Partner Rollout

DeepMind introduced ASIMOV-Jailbreak, an open benchmark of prompts testing resistance to unsafe requests. Reports indicate strong performance, up to high resistance rates when paired with Gemini 4 Argon. The model is available to partners including Agile Robots, Apptronik, and Boston Dynamics, with a waitlist for others.

## Implications for Physical AI

Gemini Robotics 2.5 advances the shift toward general models controlling robots without specialized robotics teams. Combined with factory results, it supports broader adoption of humanoids in manufacturing and logistics. Challenges remain in scaling across varied environments, integrating with existing lines, and defining responsibility for edge-case failures.

As deployments grow, economic and workflow questions will intensify. The measured 96% success in a real production setting marks a concrete step beyond laboratory proofs.

## Conclusion

With quantifiable gains and factory validation, Gemini Robotics 2.5 brings physical intelligence closer to practical use. Partner-only access limits immediate reach, but the results signal accelerating progress in embodied AI.
