# Claude Meetings Beta: Anthropic Brings AI Participants into Live Google Meet Calls

**Published: November 15, 2026**

Anthropic has launched Claude Meetings in beta, allowing Claude to join Google Meet video calls as a visible participant. Announced around November 10-12, 2026, the feature enables teams to have Claude take notes, answer questions, and create documents, whiteboards, and other artifacts in real time while the conversation is ongoing. This shifts meeting AI from post-call summarization to active collaboration during the call itself.

### How Claude Meetings Works
Users connect their Google Calendar in the Claude app or desktop client. From the Meetings section, they can add Claude to specific upcoming Google Meet sessions or enable auto-join. Once in the call, Claude appears as a named participant. Team members can speak to it directly or type in chat: for example, “Claude, start a doc for this plan” or “Claude, outline the timeline on a whiteboard.” Claude responds in the meeting chat, drafts content live, and can pull information from connected tools or apps.

After the meeting, notes, transcripts, action items, and created materials are available in claude.ai. The system saves transcripts, summaries, and screen descriptions but not audio. It is designed to leave automatically if external guests join unless admins authorize otherwise, and it prioritizes internal meetings.

### Availability and Pricing
The beta is available on Claude Team and Enterprise plans. On Team plans, it is on by default (admins can disable it); on Enterprise, admins must enable it for the organization or subsets via custom roles. Free, Pro, and Max plans do not include it yet. Support is limited to Google Meet at launch, with other platforms (Zoom, Teams, etc.) planned for later.

During the beta, notes, transcripts, and most in-call answers are included at no extra charge. Artifacts such as documents and whiteboards created in meetings are free through February 10, 2027. Use of connected tools counts toward plan usage limits. Organizations with zero data retention (ZDR), HIPAA-ready configurations, or customer-managed encryption keys (CMEK) are excluded during the beta.

### Broader Context in Workplace AI
Claude Meetings arrives as Anthropic prepares for a major IPO (targeting a valuation around $1.3–1.4 trillion, with trading expected November 19 under ticker ANT) and continues shipping model updates like Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2. It competes with existing meeting tools (Otter, Granola, Fireflies) and agent features from other labs, while Meta’s Muse has added phone-call capabilities for business interactions.

The feature emphasizes real-time productivity: a planning meeting can end with a reviewed draft rather than requiring follow-up work. Privacy controls and admin settings address enterprise concerns about data and external participants.

### Implications
For teams already using Claude and Google Meet, this reduces friction in turning discussion into deliverables. Limitations include the single-platform start, beta exclusions, and the need for consent around transcription. Success will depend on accuracy of live artifact generation, reliability in noisy calls, and expansion to more conferencing tools.

**Helpful tips:**
- Ensure all participants consent to transcription before adding Claude.
- Start with internal planning meetings to test artifact quality.
- Admins should review custom role and connector settings before broad rollout.
